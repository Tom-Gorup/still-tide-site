Help & support

Still Tide is being prepared for public iPhone release. These answers cover the current breathing and meditation app.

Email support@cybrpulse.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, app build, and a short description of what happened. Thomas Gorup handles support through this existing routed address. Include only details needed to explain the issue.

Start, pause, and finish

Choose Breathe or Meditate, choose your time, then tap the center circle. During a session, use Pause or Resume. The close button lets you end the session. Breathe comfortably; pause or stop whenever you like.

My session paused

Leaving the app, locking the phone, or an audio interruption pauses the session. Return and tap Resume when ready. Time away is not counted. After quitting the app, start a new session from home.

I cannot hear the chime

In Preferences, try Preview chime and check your media volume and connected headphones or speaker. Chimes respect Silent Mode unless you enable Play chimes in Silent Mode. Previewing does not turn on chimes for sessions.

I cannot feel the pulse

Hold your iPhone, use Preview soft pulse, and adjust Pulse strength in Preferences. Device capabilities and iPhone vibration settings can affect the result. Still Tide does not change those system settings.

Can it silence notifications?

Use Focus setup in Preferences or on the home or paused screen for instructions. You control Focus in iPhone Settings and Control Center. Still Tide cannot turn it on or guarantee silence; allowed interruptions and alarms can still occur.

Where are my sessions and settings?

There is no session history or online account. Your choices are saved locally. The privacy policy explains storage, backups, deletion, and what happens if you email support.
